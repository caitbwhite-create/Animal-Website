The Invertebrate Underworld

Invertebrates encompass a massive 95% to 97% of all animal life on Earth. Meet the tiny giants keeping the ecosystem running.

🦋 Arthropods

The largest phylum including insects, arachnids, and millipedes. Giant Hercules beetles, tarantulas, and leafcutter ants feature jointed appendages and hard chitin exoskeletons.

🐌 Mollusks

Soft-bodied land creatures like terrestrial snails and slugs. They love the high humidity levels of the rainforest floor, recycling organic decay to nourish plants.

🪱 Annelids & Nematodes

Spanning multi-segmented earthworms and land leeches that dominate damp, tropical soil layers, aerating the earth so roots can grow thick.

🕷️ Velvet Worms & Onychophorans

Fascinating, ancient jungle invertebrates characterized by soft, plump bodies and specialized slime cannons used to trap prey on the forest floor.
